Where molasses is used

A sugar by-product with many industrial buyers

Demand for molasses comes from far outside the sugar industry. These are the main uses we supply.

Ethanol and distilleries

Molasses ferments directly to ethanol with no starch conversion step. Many cassava-based ethanol plants add molasses as a second sugar source when dry cassava is scarce. Distilleries use it as a fermentation base.

Typical buyers · ethanol plants, distilleries, industrial alcohol producers

Animal feed

Pellet mills use about 2 to 5% molasses as a binder, which also cuts dust and improves taste. Ruminant diets can use about 10 to 20% of the dry matter as an energy feed. It also carries urea in liquid supplements and lick blocks.

Typical buyers · feed mills, cattle and dairy farms, block makers

Food manufacturing

Colour, body and moisture retention in sauces, pastes, baked goods and confectionery. Food use needs the right grade and documents, so tell us what your quality team requires.

Yeast and fermentation

Baker's yeast, vinegar and organic acids use molasses as a main sugar source.

Biogas and soil products

A high-sugar feedstock for biogas digesters and an ingredient in some soil-conditioning products.

Feed inclusion levels are typical figures from published feed references (Feedipedia). Your nutritionist sets the rate for your formulation.

Carbon dioxide

CO₂ and dry ice

Fermentation gives off carbon dioxide alongside ethanol. Captured, cleaned and liquefied, it is sold as beverage and industrial CO₂, and pressed into dry ice for the cold chain and for cleaning. We supply the finished product against confirmed orders.

How fermentation CO₂ becomes a product

  1. FermentationYeast turns sugar into ethanol and CO₂. In theory each tonne of ethanol comes with close to a tonne of CO₂.
  2. CaptureThe CO₂-rich gas leaving the fermenter is collected instead of vented.
  3. Clean and compressMoisture and other gases are removed and the CO₂ is compressed.
  4. Liquefy and storeThe gas is cooled into liquid CO₂ and held in an insulated cryogenic tank.
  5. Fill or pressFilled into cylinders, loaded into bulk tankers, or expanded and pressed into dry ice.
Bulk

Liquid CO₂ by tanker

Delivered by insulated tanker to your own cryogenic storage tank.

Biochar

Biochar from palm kernel shell

Biochar is a stable, carbon-rich charcoal made by heating biomass with little oxygen. We are setting up biochar supply from palm kernel shell, a plentiful residue of the palm-oil belt, and we are taking early enquiries for samples and field trials.

In development

Pilot stage. Samples and trial quantities come first, each with a laboratory test report. Commercial supply follows once production is proven.

What it is for

A soil conditioner that helps acidic, low-fertility soils hold water and nutrients. Also used in growing media and livestock bedding.

Who we want to hear from

Farms, nurseries, fertiliser blenders, researchers and carbon-project developers who want to run a trial.
